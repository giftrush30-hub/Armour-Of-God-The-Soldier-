Explore the biblical imagery behind truth, righteousness, peace, faith, salvation, and the sword of the Spirit.

  • Breastplate of Righteousness — practise what is right.
  • Shoes of Peace — move toward peace.
  • Shield of Faith — hold steady through difficulty.
  • Helmet of Salvation — remember hope.
  • Sword of the Spirit — wisdom grounded in Scripture.
